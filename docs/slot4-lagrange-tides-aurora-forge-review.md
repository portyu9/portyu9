## Optical construction (not just lines and polygons)

The light-field is composed across several independent depth planes:

1. A dark indigo/violet atmospheric chamber, distant oblique perspective
   guide rays, particulate stars and **two off-axis volumetric caustic blooms**.
2. Two counterflowing **wide lenticular ribbons** with four different
   translucent inner/outer refraction shells. One reflects icy cyan / mint,
   while the other shears into electric orchid / magenta.
3. A **static, bounded, two-octave fractal turbulence field**, clipped
   to the twin ribbons, giving the glass a pearlescent, non-flat material
   surface with no external textures or heavy frame-by-frame noise updates.
4. Many fine independent wave-filaments, microscopic crystalline facets,
   metallic seam caustics, tiny flare-crosses, etched channels, and
   spectral depth mists.
5. **Four genuine SVG path-shape morphs per surface**, with compatible
   commands across four closed-loop keyframes; four moving guided photons;
   eleven breathing micro-emitters; twin oppositely moving energy dashes
   and a faint traveling scanning glint.
6. An always-present static glass/light field. Under
   `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, all SMIL-bearing kinetic groups are
   hidden and a complete static pair of focal ribbons is restored.

The paths are mathematically composed separately for each surface instead
of relying on a GitHub CSS background trick, `<image>`, scripts, remote
resources, GIF fallback, or an embedded raster. The mobile composition
has fewer and better-spaced filaments, larger readable focal highlights,
and independent atmospheric / facet placement.
